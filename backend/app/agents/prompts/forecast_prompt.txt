You are forecasting near-term demand for a single retail product so a merchant
can decide how much to reorder.

PRODUCT
  Name:              $product_name
  Unit of measure:   $unit
  Current stock:     $current_stock $unit
  Reorder threshold: $reorder_threshold $unit
  Supplier lead time (longest option): $lead_time_days days

OBSERVED DAILY SALES (last $history_days days, oldest first)
$sales_history

YOUR TASK
Recommend how many $unit to order now.

How to think about it:
1. Read the daily figures and judge the recent demand level. Weigh recent days
   more heavily than older ones if the trend has moved.
2. Note any weekday/weekend pattern.
3. The order must cover demand during the supplier lead time AND rebuild stock
   to a healthy level above the reorder threshold. Current stock counts toward
   that cover.
4. Leave a modest buffer for variability, not a large one. Overstocking ties up
   cash and, for perishable goods, causes waste.

CONSTRAINTS
- Recommend a whole number of $unit, greater than zero.
- Base the number on the sales data given. Do not invent figures.
- If demand is erratic, say so in your reasoning and lean conservative.

RESPOND WITH EXACTLY THIS JSON OBJECT AND NOTHING ELSE
{
  "recommended_quantity": <integer>,
  "reasoning": "<2-4 sentences: the daily demand level you read from the data, how the lead time and current stock informed the number, and what buffer you left>"
}

"recommended_quantity" must be a JSON number, not a string.
Do not wrap the JSON in markdown fences. Do not add any other keys.
